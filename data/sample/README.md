# Sample data — ALL SYNTHETIC

`prospects.json` and `company_docs/` describe fictional companies and people. They exist only so the
workflows can be demonstrated offline. They are **input data**, not model output: every profile, ICP,
signal, score and email shown in the app is generated by the local model at run time.

* `prospects.json` — 9 fictional prospects (5 for Dispatchly, 4 for Ledgerline). Includes two deliberate
  poor-fit prospects, and one containing a prompt-injection attempt inside scraped text (Pixel & Pine Studio)
  to show that scraped text is treated as untrusted data.
* `conversations.json` — 12 labelled sample threads for reply classification. `expected` labels are the
  ground truth used only to report accuracy; they are never given to the model.
