# Kestrel Works — Products (SYNTHETIC SAMPLE)

## Dispatchly
Dispatch and scheduling software for field-service companies (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, solar maintenance).
Drag-and-drop scheduling, route-aware job assignment, customer arrival-window texts, technician mobile app that works
without signal, and job-completion photos. Replaces whiteboards and spreadsheets. Flat price: $49 per technician per month.
Typical buyers: operations managers, dispatch managers, owners, COOs. Typical pains: missed arrival windows, technician
windshield time, overtime from poor routing, dispatchers overloaded during seasonal peaks.

## Ledgerline
Invoice capture and approval automation for small finance teams. Reads vendor invoices from email, matches them to
purchase orders, routes approvals, flags duplicate invoices, and exports to common accounting systems.
Flat price: $299 per month for up to 5,000 invoices. Typical buyers: finance managers, controllers, heads of finance.
Typical pains: manual invoice keying, slow month-end close, duplicate payments, audit-season scramble, hiring more AP
clerks just to keep up.
